# Code of conduct

TBOSS is made by people who like music and tinkering, in their free time. Keep it a place
where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or propose an idea without being
belittled.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many people here are new to firmware or to GitHub.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any project space
  (issues, pull requests, discussions).
- Don't share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who don't follow this. To report
a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
